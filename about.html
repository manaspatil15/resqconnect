    About ResQConnect

    Built so help finds people faster than disaster does.

    ResQConnect started as a response to a simple, recurring failure: during the 2023 Kerala floods, relief supplies sat in three different NGO warehouses within 12km of camps that had run out of food — because no one had a shared view of who needed what, where. Version 1 of ResQConnect is our answer to that coordination gap.

    Our mission

    Put every citizen report, every volunteer, and every relief resource on one operating picture — so response time is measured in minutes, not days.

    Who we serve

    Citizens in affected areas, volunteers who show up to help, NGOs distributing relief, rescue teams in the field, and the administrators coordinating all four.

    What's next

    Version 1 focuses on reporting, tasking, and inventory. Real-time GPS tracking, AI damage detection, and offline mesh networking are planned for later releases.
