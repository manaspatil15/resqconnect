    Safety Guidelines

    Guidance reviewed against NDMA (National Disaster Management Authority) recommendations. Keep an emergency kit ready year-round.

    Flood

    • Move to higher ground immediately if water is rising.
    • Avoid walking or driving through moving water.
    • Turn off electricity and gas at the mains before evacuating.
    • Keep a battery radio for official updates.

    Earthquake

    • Drop, Cover, and Hold On — don't run outside during shaking.
    • Stay away from windows, mirrors, and heavy furniture.
    • If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings and poles.
    • After shaking stops, check for gas leaks before using switches.

    Cyclone

    • Secure loose objects outdoors; board up windows.
    • Stock at least 3 days of water, food, and medicine.
    • Evacuate to designated relief camps if authorities advise.
    • Stay indoors during the 'eye' of the storm — winds will resume.

    Fire

    • Stay low to avoid smoke inhalation while evacuating.
    • Feel doors before opening — do not open if hot.
    • Never use elevators during a fire evacuation.
    • Have two escape routes planned for your home or building.
    Emergency kit

    What to pack, before you need it.

    Water & food

    3 days minimum, plus a manual can opener.

    First aid & medicine

    Prescription medicines, bandages, antiseptic.

    Documents

    ID proof, insurance papers in a waterproof pouch.

    Power & light

    Torch, power bank, spare batteries.
